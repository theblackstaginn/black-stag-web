Southern Pressure Washing Website Case Study

Southern Pressure Washing case study: contractor website with service-area pages, estimate requests, before-and-after galleries, video, FAQs, and local SEO.

Business type: Pressure washing & mobile auto detailing · Location: Northeast Georgia & North Metro Atlanta

View the live site Contractor web design

The challenge

A contractor website has one primary job: make it easy for a local customer to see proof, understand the service area, and request an estimate.

Southern Pressure Washing serves multiple Georgia markets and offers both exterior cleaning and mobile auto detailing. The site needed to explain that range without burying the estimate request.

Because the work is highly visual, project proof also needed to be a major part of the experience.

  • Clear service and service-area navigation
  • Fast estimate-request path
  • Before-and-after project proof
  • Pressure-washing and auto-detailing content
  • Local-business search structure

The build

The site is organized around the questions a contractor customer asks before making contact.

Dedicated Services, Service Areas, FAQ, and Gallery pages support the homepage instead of forcing every keyword and proof point into one page.

The homepage keeps the request-an-estimate action prominent, then backs it up with work-truck photography, before-and-after carousels, project galleries, service lists, video, and a direct call option.

  • Estimate-request integration
  • Dedicated services and service-area pages
  • Before-and-after image carousels
  • Full project gallery
  • Mobile auto-detailing section
  • Work videos and visual proof
  • LocalBusiness structured data and canonical URLs

Why this structure fits contractor SEO

Local contractor search is usually a combination of service + place + proof.

Separating services, areas, FAQ content, and galleries gives search engines clearer page topics while also making the site easier for a real customer to navigate.

The structure can expand with additional service-area or service-detail pages as the business grows without rebuilding the whole site.

The result

Southern Pressure Washing now has a dedicated lead-focused website instead of a single generic contractor landing page.

Visitors can see real work, understand the service range, confirm the areas served, and move directly into an estimate request or phone call.

The company also owns a portable website foundation that can continue accumulating local content and project proof over time.
