Stag & Stone Coffee and Bakehouse Website Case Study

Stag & Stone case study: a multi-page coffee and bakehouse site with responsive menus, event-service pages, custom imagery, and owned-code architecture.

Business type: Coffee shop, bakehouse & mobile event service · Location: Clarkesville, Georgia

View the live site Restaurant & food web design

The challenge

Stag & Stone is more than a coffee menu, so the website had to explain several connected parts of the business without flattening the brand.

The project needed to introduce the coffee and bakehouse concept while also giving the coffee cart, mobile tavern service, menu, and bakehouse their own clear paths.

The visual direction also had to carry an old-world Appalachian character without sacrificing speed or readability on phones.

  • Strong brand presentation
  • Readable food and drink menus
  • Dedicated bakehouse content
  • Coffee-cart event inquiries
  • Mobile-tavern service information
  • A structure that can grow with the storefront

The build

The site became a small multi-page brand system rather than a single promotional page.

Separate pages handle the menu, bakehouse, coffee cart, and mobile tavern so visitors can go directly to the part of the business they care about.

Custom imagery, responsive menu architecture, and a consistent visual language keep those pages feeling like one place while still giving each service room to explain itself.

  • Responsive multi-page architecture
  • Menu and bakehouse pages
  • Coffee-cart and mobile-tavern service pages
  • Email-based event inquiry calls to action
  • Custom icons, imagery, and brand-specific interface elements
  • Manifest support for an app-like mobile experience

Built for expansion

The site was designed around a business that is still growing.

Because the content is split into focused pages, Stag & Stone can add new menu categories, storefront information, events, or service details without turning the homepage into a wall of text.

The owned-code structure also keeps the business free to change hosting or development support later without rebuilding the brand from scratch.

The result

The website now presents Stag & Stone as a complete hospitality brand instead of only a coffee cart or a menu.

Visitors can understand the core concept, browse the food and drink offering, explore the bakehouse, and find the appropriate event service without leaving the branded site.

The project also serves as a reusable digital foundation for the storefront as the business expands.
