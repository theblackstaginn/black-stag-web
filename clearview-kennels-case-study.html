Clearview Kennels Website Case Study

Clearview Kennels case study: breeder website with puppy listings, individual profiles, applications, mobile navigation, SEO, and installable web-app features.

Business type: Cavalier King Charles Spaniel & Cavapoo breeder · Location: Marshfield, Missouri

View the live site Small-business web design

The challenge

A breeder site has to do more than look pretty. Families need to understand the program, see available puppies, ask questions, and move into an application without getting lost.

Clearview Kennels needed a cleaner digital storefront for its Cavalier King Charles Spaniels and Cavapoos, with a structure that could keep working as puppies and availability changed over time.

The site also needed to feel personal and trustworthy without turning every update into a full website rebuild.

  • Clear breed and program information
  • Current puppy discovery
  • Individual puppy pages
  • A straightforward application path
  • Mobile-first navigation for families browsing on phones

The build

The finished site uses a multi-page architecture instead of forcing the entire breeder experience into one overloaded homepage.

The project includes a homepage, puppy listing page, individual puppy detail view, and application page. Puppy data is separated from the page layout so listings and profiles can be updated without redesigning the whole site.

The navigation and calls to action were built around the way prospective families actually move through the decision: learn about the breeder, meet the puppies, open a specific profile, then apply or get in touch.

  • Data-driven puppy listings and filters
  • Individual puppy profile pages
  • Application and contact workflow
  • Responsive image treatment and mobile navigation
  • Structured metadata, sitemap, robots file, and canonical URLs
  • Installable web-app support for returning visitors

Why the architecture matters

Breeder websites change constantly. Puppies become available, are reserved, and move to their families.

Separating the puppy data from the presentation keeps those routine changes manageable. The core site design does not have to be rebuilt every time the available-puppy list changes.

That same separation also makes the site easier to grow later with more profiles, parent information, FAQ content, or other breeder resources.

The result

Clearview now has a dedicated website that supports the entire puppy-discovery path instead of relying on scattered social posts.

Families can learn about the program, browse puppies, open a specific puppy profile, and move into the application process from the same branded experience.

The site is also built on owned files and a custom domain, so the breeder is not tied to a page-builder subscription to keep the storefront online.
